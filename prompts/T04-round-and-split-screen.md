# T04 - The round, scoring and split-screen (Sat 14 Nov) - agent: Claude Code

## Goal
A complete 4-minute match: catch fish, carry them in your boat, bank them at your dock; most banked fish wins.

## In scope
- Fish spots (bubbling circles) that move around; idle in one for 3 s to land a fish (it flops into your crate).
- Fish ride in the BOAT, not the player: whoever drives the boat to their dock banks them. Hard rams spill fish.
- Each crew has a dock with a coloured flag. Scoreboard: banked fish per crew; timer; final 30 s announcer sting.
- End screen: winner, banked fish, and comedy stats (times overboard, boats stolen, longest swim).
- Local split-screen 2P (gamepads): P2 replaces one AI crew. Main menu: Solo vs AI / 2P vs AI.

## Acceptance (manual)
- [ ] A first-time player understands the goal within 20 s without reading more than one line.
- [ ] Rounds end in a close-ish score at least half the time.
